# Generator matrix, 2026-10-28 (gas-ice-docs, Stage 6)

Every generator × carbon mode {`c4_only`, `coupled`} × `pore_gas_fate` {`retain`, `to_ocean`} ×
initial ocean {pure water, `cometary_ice_ocean()`} × porewater temperature {25, 100, 160 °C}, for
1 orbit, PHREEQC 3.8.6, the Enceladus profile (porewater 261.2 atm, ocean 66.7 atm). Fate and
ocean apply to the tidal generators only; the bulk generators run in porewater mode (W:R 1). The
kinetic generators use 15 wt% IOM and `trace_C_molal = 1e-14`; the equilibrium generators are
inorganic. Restarts continue the 1-orbit `tidal_kinetic` / `bulk_kinetic` runs for 1 more orbit.
Runtimes are wall-clock with 14–18 runs sharing 22 cores, so they overstate a lone run several-fold
(a lone 2-orbit 160 °C `tidal_kinetic` takes 140–290 s).

**Results.** All `tidal_kinetic`, `bulk_kinetic`, `bulk_equil` and restart cases converge. The
cometary `tidal_equil` cases first failed in every combination ("Cl has not converged"): their
porewater had no carbon, and `tidal_equil` had no seed option. With `trace_C_molal = 1e-14` (added
to `tidal_equil` for this) they run, **except `to_ocean` + `c4_only`, where PHREEQC 3.8.6 crashes**
(segmentation fault in the ocean step, during its convergence retries) at every temperature.
`tidal_kinetic` runs that combination. Every run also prints the start-up notices "Element X is
contained in Y (which has 0.0 mass)", ~100 of the warnings counted.

**Final count (2026-10-29).** The table's 102 rows are 90 distinct cases plus 12 reruns: the 12
cometary `tidal_equil` cases rerun with `trace_C_molal = 1e-14`. With the seeded reruns in place
of the unseeded attempts, **87 of the 90 cases converge** (78 + 9). The 3 that fail are the
`tidal_equil` + `to_ocean` + cometary + `c4_only` segfaults at 25, 100 and 160 °C. (The "87 of
102" line under the table counts rows.)

**The segfault.** A 20-line reproducer is in `tests/known_failures/` (README there). It was reduced
automatically from the 25 °C input and crashes PHREEQC 3.8.6 with the stock `llnl.dat`. Its two
steps: a solution equilibrated with Fe metal, albite and Chamosite-7A; then MIX of a small
fraction of it with a KINETICS reactant that adds the pore gas's H₂ (`GET`). Without the chlorite,
or without the KINETICS, it runs.

**Where the unseeded cases' Cl comes from.** The "Cl has not converged. Total: 1.912500e-02" is
the porewater's lawrencite: those inputs have `Lawrencite 0 0.0095625 dissolve_only`, and FeCl₂
gives 2 × 0.0095625 = 0.019125 mol Cl. The cometary ocean has no Cl. With the seed the same Cl
converges, so the failure is in solving the carbon-free porewater, with Cl as the element that
fails to converge.

**The CO₂-only stall (cometary bisection, 10-28; rerun 10-29).** To find which cometary component
made the carbon-free porewater fail, `tidal_kinetic` (100 °C, 1 orbit, no IOM, `c4_only`,
`retain`) was run with the cometary REACTION reduced to one component. The ocean is then 0.0293
mol CO₂ alone:
- unseeded: no progress past the first porewater REACT step. It was killed at the 3,000 s limit;
  `run.out` had not changed since the first second;
- with `trace_C_molal = 1e-14`: completes in 267 s, 0 errors.

The full cometary ocean without the seed is slow but not stuck: 1,459 s, 0 errors, run alongside
other jobs, against 394 s seeded. The earlier "> 20 min without converging" (CHANGELOG, Stage 3)
was a timeout. In the same bisection, the Mtg-only (CH₄ and C₂H₆) and O₂-only oceans failed ("Numerical
method failed on all combinations of convergence parameters", MIX 4); the H₂S-only, N₂-only and
"O"-only (the −O that turns CO into CO₂) oceans completed. Not understood; the seed is the workaround, and both generators warn without it.
