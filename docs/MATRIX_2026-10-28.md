# Generator matrix, 2026-10-28 (gas-ice-docs, Stage 6)

Every generator × carbon mode {`c4_only`, `coupled`} × `pore_gas_fate` {`retain`, `to_ocean`} ×
initial ocean {pure water, `cometary_ice_ocean()`} × porewater temperature {25, 100, 160 °C}, for
1 orbit, PHREEQC 3.8.6, the Enceladus profile (porewater 261.2 atm, ocean 66.7 atm). Fate and
ocean apply to the tidal generators only; the bulk generators run in porewater mode (W:R 1). The
kinetic generators use 15 wt% IOM and `trace_C_molal = 1e-14`; the equilibrium generators are
inorganic. Restarts continue the 1-orbit `tidal_kinetic` / `bulk_kinetic` runs for 1 more orbit.
Runtimes are wall-clock with 14–18 runs sharing 22 cores, so they overstate a lone run several-fold
(a lone 2-orbit 160 °C `tidal_kinetic` takes 140–290 s).

**Results.** All `tidal_kinetic`, `bulk_kinetic`, `bulk_equil` and restart cases converge. The
cometary `tidal_equil` cases first failed in every combination ("Cl has not converged"): their
porewater had no carbon, and `tidal_equil` had no seed option. With `trace_C_molal = 1e-14` (added
to `tidal_equil` for this) they run, **except `to_ocean` + `c4_only`, where PHREEQC 3.8.6 crashes**
(segmentation fault in the ocean step, during its convergence retries) at every temperature.
`tidal_kinetic` runs that combination. Every run also prints the start-up notices "Element X is
contained in Y (which has 0.0 mass)", ~100 of the warnings counted.
