**[V] Critical scope caveat, applies to the entire dataset:** all Casey
(1993) experiments were run rigorously ANOXIC by design -- N2-degassed,
O2 scrubbed to <6e-6 mol/L via Fe filings/hot Cu, CO2 stripped with
NaOH. The paper states Mn2+ oxidation to Mn3+/Mn4+ "severely retards"
dissolution. This rate law is therefore valid ONLY under reducing
conditions consistent with this project's Scenario R reduced-precursor
assumption (§1) -- it does NOT transfer to any oxidizing scenario
without correction, and should not be reused elsewhere in this project
(e.g. any future oxidized/weathered comparison case) without that
caveat restated.
